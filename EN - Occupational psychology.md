Psychologie du travail.


Occupational psychology: studies human behavior, thoughts and feelings in the workplace and applies psychological theories and methods to improve how people work and how organizations function. 

The dual client problem: an occupational psychologist has 2 customers, whose interests don't necessarily align. 

Psychological assessment at work. 

Chartership: professionnalisme
"Organisational psychologist" or "business psychologist"

Initialism: on dit les lettres distinctement, une après l'autre (CEO)
Acronym: on lit les lettres ensemble, comme un mot (NATO)
